Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the App Revenue mobile application (the “App”) collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the App, you agree to this policy.

1. Who we are

  • App: App Revenue
  • Developer: Orion Dijital (Enes Ünal)
  • Contact: app.revenue.tr@gmail.com

2. Summary (in plain language)

No personal accounts · No personal analytics

  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, or precise location.
  • We use Google AdMob to display ads, which uses your device’s advertising identifier.
  • We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions and purchases.
  • The App shows estimates about other apps using public store data; we do not collect personal data about those apps or their users.

3. Information collected

a) Advertising data — via Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may process your device advertising identifier (e.g., Android Advertising ID, or Apple IDFA where you allow it), general device information, IP address, and ad-interaction data. This processing is performed by Google.

b) Purchase & subscription data — via RevenueCat. To unlock and restore premium features, we process purchase receipts/tokens, product identifiers, subscription status, and an anonymous, app-generated user identifier. We do not receive your payment card details — payments are handled by Apple App Store or Google Play.

c) On-device usage data. Your daily free-query count and subscription flag are stored locally on your device and are not transmitted to us.

We do not collect: name, email, phone, contacts, precise location, health data, or behavioral profiling/analytics.

4. How information is used

  • To provide core functionality (showing app download & revenue estimates).
  • To display and measure advertisements (free version).
  • To process and restore subscriptions/purchases and remove ads for subscribers.
  • To enforce free usage limits and prevent abuse.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

5. Legal bases (where GDPR applies)

  • Consent — for personalized advertising.
  • Contract — to provide subscriptions you purchase.
  • Legitimate interests — security and abuse prevention.

6. Third parties & data sharing

We share the limited data described above only with service providers that make the App work:

  • Google AdMob (advertising) — Google Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat (subscription management) — RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store / Google Play (payment processing) — governed by their respective policies.

We do not sell your personal data.

7. Public data about other apps

App Revenue displays publicly available store information (such as ratings, reviews, and listing details) about third-party applications and produces estimates of downloads and revenue. This is public, non-personal information; we do not gather personal data about those apps’ developers or users.

8. Advertising & your choices

  • You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).
  • On iOS, ad tracking is subject to App Tracking Transparency (ATT) consent.
  • Purchasing any subscription removes ads.

9. Data retention

  • Advertising data: retained by Google per its policies.
  • Purchase/subscription data: retained by RevenueCat and the app stores as long as needed for subscription management and legal/accounting requirements.
  • On-device data: retained until you clear the app’s data or uninstall the App.

10. Security

We and our providers apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

11. Children’s privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us at app.revenue.tr@gmail.com and we will take appropriate action.

12. International data transfers

Our providers (e.g., Google, RevenueCat) may process data in countries outside your own, including the United States, under appropriate safeguards.

13. Your rights

Depending on your location (e.g., GDPR/EEA, UK, or CCPA/California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or object to processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. Because most data is held by Google and RevenueCat, we will help direct your request. Contact app.revenue.tr@gmail.com.
